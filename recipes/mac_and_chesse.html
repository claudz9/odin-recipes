Mac and Cheese

Description

Mac and cheese is a beloved comfort food that combines the simplicity of pasta with the indulgence of melted cheese. This creamy, satisfying dish is perfect for a quick weeknight meal or a special occasion. The combination of perfectly cooked pasta and smooth, rich cheese sauce creates a truly satisfying meal that is sure to please everyone at the table.

Baked until golden and bubbly, this traditional mac and cheese is a testament to the beauty of simple, quality ingredients combined with patience and care. Whether you're a seasoned cook or trying this recipe for the first time, homemade mac and cheese is an achievable and rewarding dish that brings people together.

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces elbow macaroni
  • 2 cups shredded sharp cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 3 cups milk
  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 2 1/2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1/2 cup bread crumbs
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Cook the macaroni according to package instructions.
  2. In a large saucepan, melt the butter and whisk in the flour.
  3. Gradually add the milk, stirring constantly until smooth.
  4. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 2-3 minutes.
  5. Remove from heat and stir in the shredded cheddar cheese and Parmesan cheese until melted.
  6. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  7. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  8. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish.
  9. Layer half of the cooked noodles over the sauce.
  10. Spread half of the ricotta cheese mixture over the noodles.
  11. Layer half of the mozzarella cheese over the ricotta.
  12. Repeat the layers once more.
  13. Top with the remaining Parmesan cheese.
  14. Bake for 30 minutes or until golden brown and bubbly.
