Potato Bake

Description

This Easy Creamy Cheesy Potato Bake is true to its name—easy, creamy, and cheesy! Made with cubes of potato cooked with the sauce, there's no need to par boil the potatoes or make the sauce separately. Plus, it's faster to chop potatoes than slice them thinly, and you get more sauce in every bite!

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (28 ounces) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley

steps

  1. Cook lasagna noodles according to package instructions.
  2. In a large skillet, brown the ground beef with the chopped onion and garlic.
  3. Add the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, basil, oregano, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  5. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish.
  6. Layer half of the cooked noodles over the sauce.
  7. Spread half of the ricotta cheese mixture over the noodles.
  8. Layer half of the mozzarella cheese over the ricotta.
  9. Repeat the layers once more.
  10. Top with the remaining Parmesan cheese.
  11. Bake for 30 minutes or until golden brown and bubbly.
