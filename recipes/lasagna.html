Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish that layers sheets of pasta with rich meat sauce, creamy béchamel, and melted cheese. This beloved comfort food is perfect for family dinners and special occasions. The combination of perfectly cooked noodles, savory meat ragù, and smooth cheese sauce creates a truly satisfying meal that is sure to please everyone at the table.

Baked until golden and bubbly, this traditional lasagna is a testament to the beauty of simple, quality ingredients combined with patience and care. Whether you're a seasoned cook or trying this recipe for the first time, homemade lasagna is an achievable and rewarding dish that brings people together.
